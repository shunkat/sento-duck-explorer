Final export: 0.36 × 0.30 × 0.36 m, bottom-center origin, +Y up / +Z front; 3,000 triangles, one ivory material, no textures, 55,568 bytes. The four legs, central drain and underside clearance are retained. The lower 1,200-triangle draft damaged contours; the provisional budget was increased to 3,000.
