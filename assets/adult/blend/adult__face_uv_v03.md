# Adult face UV correction, v03

This free Blender correction derives from the preserved `adult__v02.blend`, which retains the original Meshy 6 Lite source and image provenance. Geometry, topology, transferred normals and the `reference_position` attribute are unchanged. The two source-derived outfits remain 5,800 triangles each, with one embedded 1,024 × 1,024 base-color texture per outfit. The derivative remains CC BY 4.0. This intermediate source is ready for the separate human rigging stage; it does not itself contain walking animation.

The old face UV selection used posed coordinates even though Adult's pose shifts depth by 0.10 m. Consequently, 76 of 126 inspected eye-region triangles in the clothed outfit and 77 of 135 in the bath outfit were outside the high-density face region. Small disconnected body UV islands caused inconsistent facial resolution and visible blurry eye edges.

`scripts/repair-adult-face.py` selects the complete front head in preserved source coordinates and unfolds that connected region with Angle Based UV unwrapping. The unfolded face is kept separate from the general body atlas and receives a supersampled emission bake reduced to the existing 1,024-square output. A first orthographic-projection experiment was rejected because folded ears and hair overlapped; it is not the accepted candidate. The ear skin mask also follows the complete ear attachment, removing the exposed triangular hair-color patches without changing the mesh.

Reproduce from the repository root with Blender's `--background --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/repair-adult-face.py`. It writes this editable source and review evidence, and a private candidate GLB; it never overwrites `public/models`. `-- --audit-only` rechecks the saved source and private candidate without rebaking.

The producer asserts identical position, topology, corner-normal and reference-attribute hashes before/after repair, complete inspected-eye UV coverage, and zero face-patch overlaps sampled at the 1,024 pixel centers. It also reopens the saved Blend, verifies packed images, checks a private uncompressed copy and GLB for common private-path/email/token patterns, and strips embedded PNG metadata. These are scoped checks, not an exhaustive confidentiality guarantee or a mathematical proof between sample points.

`../quality/face-uv-v03/measurements.json` records exact hashes and counts; `uv-and-metadata-audit.json` records the sampled coverage and metadata result. The front, side, three-quarter and face images for both outfits are rendered after reimporting the actual candidate GLB. Normal-input gameplay and GPU rendering in the actual game are separate integration checks. This revision retains the stylized source's simplified facial geometry and hand anatomy rather than claiming a replacement sculpt.
