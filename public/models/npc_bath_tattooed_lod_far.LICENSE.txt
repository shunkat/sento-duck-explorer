Derived from the project's independent standing visitor distance model.
The original distant geometry, oriented normals, pose and bounds are preserved;
vertices split only for new UV charts. Intended beyond 6m camera-to-center or
in Overview, with the same tattoo atlas as the near visitor.

The original koi, wave and peony artwork was generated from a project text
prompt with the built-in OpenAI image tool through Codex, without third-party
image inputs. Blender composes it into a 1024px atlas with the existing eye
region retained at native resolution. Project-held art and modification rights
are MIT to the extent permitted by law. The original geometry remains CC0.
