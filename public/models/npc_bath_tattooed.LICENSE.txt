The project's independent standing visitor supplies the body, eyes, eyebrows
and hairstyle. This appearance variant retains its exact oriented geometry,
normals, pose and bounds, with vertex splits only for new UV charts.
It contains no geometry or textures from the playable father.

Original koi, wave and peony tattoo artwork was generated from a project text
prompt with the built-in OpenAI image tool through Codex. No third-party image
was supplied. Blender material composition creates a 1024px atlas retaining
the original eye's native pixel resolution. Project-held rights in the art and
UV/material modifications are released under MIT to the extent permitted by law.
